About

Your signed-in employee space.

P

PeopleDesk employee space

This mobile-first frontend is a prototype for attendance, leave requests, and work-from-home requests.

  • Sign in with the User ID and password created in Settings → Users.
  • Attendance is validated and stored by the backend. Leave/WFH requests remain local drafts, scoped to your account on this device.
  • Phone browsers cannot verify the Wi-Fi network name.
  • Face verification is not implemented and does not access the camera.
